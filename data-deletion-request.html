Request Account & Data Deletion

If you wish to delete your Saparya account and all associated personal data (including your name, email, and order history), please follow the instructions below.

How to Request Deletion:

Send an email from your registered email address to saparyacustomercare@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

What data will be deleted?

  • Personal Identifiers (Name, Email, Phone Number)
  • Delivery Addresses and Priest Booking History
  • Purchase History and account preferences

Note: Once processed, this action cannot be undone. We will fulfill your request within 7 business days.
