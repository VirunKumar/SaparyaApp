Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Welcome to Saparya. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle your information when you purchase Pooja kits or book priest services via our application.

1. Information We Collect

To fulfill your orders and bookings, we collect Personal Details (such as your name, email, and phone number) and Location Data (the physical address required for the delivery of items or arrival of a priest).

2. Payment Security

For online transactions, we use Razorpay. We do not store your credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers. For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, your contact details are used solely to facilitate the transaction upon arrival.

3. Data Deletion & Retention

We retain your information as long as your account remains active. You possess the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of your personal data at any time. For instructions, please visit our Data Deletion Page.
