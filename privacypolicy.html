Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Welcome to Saparya. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle your information when you purchase Pooja kits, book priest services, or buy individual items through our app.

1. Information We Collect

To fulfill your orders and bookings, we collect the following:

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, and phone number.
  • Location Data: Physical address for delivery of items or for a priest to arrive for a service.
  • Transaction Data: Details about the products or services you have purchased.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly for business operations, including:

  • Processing and shipping your Pooja kits and individual items.
  • Coordinating with priests to fulfill your service bookings.
  • Sending order confirmations and service updates.

3. Payment Security (Razorpay & COD)

For online payments, we use Razorpay. We do not store your credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers; this data is handled securely by Razorpay. For Cash on Delivery (COD), we use your contact details solely to facilitate the physical transaction.

4. Data Sharing

We only share your information with necessary third parties like logistics partners (for delivery) and service providers (priests) who need your address to perform rituals.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your information as long as your account is active. You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data at any time. You can visit our Data Deletion Page for instructions.
